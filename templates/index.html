Why is Human-Assisted AI better than pure AI generators?

No doubt AI is generating content but not satisfying 100%. Pure AI generators write stock copy, generate broken layouts, and require coding skills to host. We use AI to speed up drafting and coding, but **real human developers manually audit, polish, and custom-code every pixel** to make sure your site looks premium and actually works.

Do I truly own the website?

Yes. Once the code is approved, we give you the source zip file (HTML, CSS, JS) and own it 100%. No platform lock-ins or hidden contracts. You can download it and host it anywhere in the world.

What happens if I need changes later?

You can log into your portal dashboard at any time to open a ticket. Minor text or image tweaks are free during setup. For ongoing updates after launch, you can hire us on a flexible minor maintenance retainer or update the clean code files yourself.

Can I use my existing domain name?

Yes. During setup, you can provide your domain name. We provide the exact DNS records to configure in your GoDaddy, Namecheap, or Google Domains registrar to route your traffic securely with free SSL.
