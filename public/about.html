The Centaur Model (Manual + AI)

In chess, a **Centaur** is a team combining a human player and a computer engine. By combining the two, they make fewer mistakes than a machine or human playing alone.

We apply this exact philosophy to web design. Our **AI coordinator** handles the requirements gathering, copy drafting, and outline mapping so you don't need a technical spec writer. Then, our **manual developer network** takes over to hand-craft high-performance HTML, CSS, and JS. No bloated site builders. Pure, fast, clean code.
